The Verdict

This wasn't one problem. The funnel broke at every stage.

The webinar failed because of compounding breakdowns across the entire funnel: ads stopped running 9 days before the event, the email invitation to non-buyers got 3 clicks out of 979, only 32 of 982 invited people showed up live, and the pitch asked a €50 audience to spend €497–1,497 on products that didn't fully exist yet. This is the same pattern as August 4 (350 attendees, 10 sales) — but with far fewer attendees reaching the room.

Root Cause Analysis

The Seven Breaks

Critical

Ads stopped running 9 days before the webinar

The main campaign spent meaningfully only Aug 15–18, then dropped to near-zero. On webinar day, only RON 2.47 was spent (83 impressions, zero clicks). The traffic campaign ("META_Traffic_RO_Webinar_27aug") never delivered a single impression — €0 total spend. Total ad investment was ~€62 for a launch targeting €6,000+ in revenue.

Critical

10–30× price jump with no trust bridge

The audience's highest price point was €50 (AI course). The webinar pitched €497 / €997 / €1,497 packages — products that didn't fully exist yet ("founding cohort" of 12 AI systems, video walkthroughs, live sessions). No testimonials, case studies, or proof of results existed for these offers. Only the €47 Challenge had a price the audience recognized, and it got exactly 1 click.

Critical

18+ offer variants have exhausted the audience

Your own September Growth Reset doc diagnosed this: "The buyer doesn't know what to choose first." The same audience has seen: €24 kit, €39 package, €47 challenge, €50/87/97/125/147 AI courses, €27/57/97 communities, €59 English AI, €150 sessions, €250 Done-for-You, plus B2B offers. Price changed faster than perceived value. The result is decision paralysis and eroded trust.

Critical

Only 32 people attended live

Of 982 subscribers tagged for the webinar invitation, only 32 showed up. The main invitation email to non-buyers (979 people, Aug 18) got a 0.31% click rate — 3 clicks total. By comparison, the Aug 4 webinar had 350 attendees. Registration was through Zoom only, so there's no Kit data on who even registered vs. who was just emailed.

High

Confusing promotion: paid → free → upsell

The 8 promotional scripts (Aug 12) sold the webinar as a €50 paid event: "50 EUR, with both AI courses included." But the Aug 27 morning email called it "webinar live gratuit" (free). Then during the webinar, attendees were pitched €497–1,497 packages. This triple pivot — paid to free to upsell — confuses the value proposition at every stage.

High

No post-webinar follow-up sequence

The only follow-up was one sales email and a WhatsApp invitation, both sent the same evening. There is no replay email, no objection-handling sequence, no deadline reminder for the 48-hour founder pricing. Most webinar sales happen in the 24–72 hour follow-up window, which was completely missed.

Medium

Two competing business models on the same audience

The B2C creator funnel (courses, challenges, communities for job seekers) and the B2B Yago Automations service (€1,500–5,000 implementations for recruitment agencies) run in parallel on the same channels. The "START HERE" doc in Notion says "ignore every other package" and focus on B2B for 30 days — but the webinar went ahead anyway.

Infrastructure Gaps

What Wasn't Set Up

Gap

No webinar registration form in Kit

Registration was Zoom-only, so there's no data on who registered vs. who just received emails. Impossible to measure registration → attendance conversion or send targeted reminders.

Gap

No analytics infrastructure

Windsor.ai has zero connectors set up. No GA4, no Stripe, no Meta Ads data flowing to any analytics dashboard. Landing page performance, conversion rates, and drop-off points are invisible.

Gap

Sales tracked nowhere centrally

Kit Commerce only knows about 4 total €50 purchases. The €497–1,497 offers go through Stripe. The €47 challenge goes through Beacons. Airtable has targets but no actuals. No single source of truth for revenue.

Gap

P0 checklist items left unchecked

The September Growth Reset listed critical pre-webinar tasks that weren't completed: yAGO Pro activation, removing old conflicting prices from Beacons, completing Terms/Privacy for yAGO, and testing the full purchase flow end-to-end.

The Council's Recommendations

What to Do Next

1. Stop launching and simplify the offer

Your own Growth Reset doc has the right diagnosis. Kill every product except one clear ladder: Lead Magnet (free) → 5-Day Challenge (€47) → AI Course (€147). Remove the €24 kit, €39 package, €59 English course, and €50 AI Operator page from Beacons today. One offer, one price, one CTA. The audience needs 90 days of consistency before a higher-ticket launch.

2. Don't attempt €497+ until you have proof

A €50 audience will not jump to €497 without social proof, case studies, and a trust bridge. Build the bridge: run 3–5 cohorts of the €47 Challenge, collect testimonials and results, then create a natural upgrade path. The "founding cohort" model requires either a large warm audience or a personal-selling approach (DMs, calls), not a webinar blast.

3. Fix the ads before the next launch

Investigate why the campaign stopped delivering after Aug 18 (budget exhaustion? audience saturation? disapproved ads?). The "Flavia Popescu" ad account needs to be reinstated. For the next event: set a promotion period of 14+ days with consistent daily budget, test creatives in the first 3 days, and don't let the ads go dark in the final week.

4. Build a real follow-up sequence

Create a 5–7 email post-webinar sequence in Kit: replay link (Day 1), case study/testimonial (Day 2), objection handling (Day 3), deadline reminder (Day 4), final call (Day 5). Most webinar revenue comes from follow-up, not the live pitch. The single same-night email left 95% of potential recovery on the table.

5. Set up tracking infrastructure

Connect Stripe, Meta Ads, and GA4 to Windsor.ai (or a simple Google Sheet via Zapier). Create a Kit registration form for every event so you can track registrations → attendance → sales. You cannot improve what you cannot measure.

6. Pick one business model for the next 90 days

B2C creator (courses/challenges to job seekers) and B2B service (Yago automation for agencies) are different businesses with different audiences. Running both dilutes both. The Notion "START HERE" doc recommends B2B for 30 days — either commit to that or commit to the simplified B2C ladder. Don't split energy.
