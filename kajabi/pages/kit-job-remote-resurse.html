Template de CV compatibil ATS

Recrutorii internationali citesc CV-ul in engleza. Sistemele ATS citesc doar text simplu. Reguli: o coloana, fara tabele, fara poze, fara grafice, font Arial sau Calibri 10 la 12, maxim 2 pagini, salvat ca PDF cu numele Prenume_Nume_CV.pdf.

Copiaza structura de mai jos intr-un document Google Docs sau Word si inlocuieste textul dintre paranteze.

FIRSTNAME LASTNAME
(Target role, ex: Customer Success Manager) | Remote | (City, Romania)
(email) | (phone with +40) | linkedin.com/in/(your-url)

SUMMARY
(Target role) with (X) years of experience in (industry). I help (type of company) (result you bring), for example (one number: grew retention by 18%, handled 60 tickets a day, cut onboarding time from 10 to 6 days). Fluent in English, used daily with clients from (countries).

SKILLS
(Skill 1) | (Skill 2) | (Skill 3) | (Tool 1) | (Tool 2) | (Tool 3)
Tip: copy 6 to 10 skills word for word from the job ad you apply to.

EXPERIENCE
(Job title) | (Company) | (City or Remote) | (MM/YYYY) to (MM/YYYY or Present)
- (Action verb) + (what you did) + (result with a number)
- Example: Reduced customer response time from 24h to 4h by building 30 email templates
- Example: Onboarded 45 new B2B clients in 6 months, with 96% satisfaction score
- (3 to 5 bullets per role, newest role first)

(Previous job title) | (Company) | (MM/YYYY) to (MM/YYYY)
- (2 to 4 bullets, same formula)

EDUCATION
(Degree) | (University) | (Year)

CERTIFICATIONS
(Certification name) | (Issuer) | (Year)
Tip: add free AI certifications from your 5 certificari AI guide.

LANGUAGES
Romanian (native) | English (C1, professional) | (Other)

Test final: deschide jobul la care aplici, cauta 5 cuvinte cheie din anunt si verifica daca apar in CV. Daca lipsesc, adauga-le in Summary si Skills.

3

Checklist LinkedIn in 15 pasi

  1. Poza: fata ta, fundal simplu, lumina naturala, zambet. Ocupa 60% din cadru.
  2. Banner: rolul tau tinta si o propozitie despre ce rezolvi. Il faci gratuit in Canva.
  3. Headline in engleza: Rol tinta | Industrie | Rezultat. Exemplu: Customer Success Manager | SaaS | Helping B2B clients stay and grow.
  4. Seteaza Open to Work vizibil doar pentru recrutori, cu rolurile tinta si Remote bifat.
  5. Locatie: orasul tau, Romania. Recrutorii filtreaza dupa tara.
  6. URL personalizat: linkedin.com/in/prenume-nume.
  7. About: 3 paragrafe scurte. Cine esti, ce rezultate ai adus cu cifre, ce cauti acum. Ultima linie: emailul tau.
  8. Experience: aceleasi bullet-uri ca in CV, cu cifre. Profilul si CV-ul spun aceeasi poveste.
  9. Skills: adauga 30 de skill-uri si pune in top 3 pe cele din anunturile tinta.
  10. Featured: un proiect, un articol sau un certificat. Ceva concret de aratat.
  11. Certificari: trece-le in Licenses and certifications.
  12. Recomandari: cere 2 de la fosti colegi sau sefi. Le dai tu un draft scurt ca sa le fie usor.
  13. Limba profilului: engleza. Adauga o versiune secundara in romana doar daca aplici si local.
  14. Conexiuni: trimite 10 cereri pe zi catre recrutori si oameni din companiile tinta, cu o nota scurta.
  15. Activitate: un comentariu util pe zi la postarile din industria ta. Recrutorii vad activitatea.

4

10 mesaje catre recrutori

Scrie in engleza. Maxim 300 de caractere pentru cererea de conexiune. Inlocuieste textul dintre paranteze. Personalizeaza mereu prima propozitie.

1. CERERE DE CONEXIUNE CATRE RECRUTOR
Hi (Name), I saw you hire for (team) at (Company). I am a (role) with (X) years in (industry) and I am exploring remote roles. Would love to connect.

2. DUPA CE A ACCEPTAT CONEXIUNEA
Thanks for connecting, (Name). I noticed the (Job title) role at (Company). I have (one result with a number) in a similar role. Is this position still open? Happy to send my CV.

3. DUPA CE AI APLICAT
Hi (Name), I just applied for (Job title) at (Company). The part about (detail from the ad) matches what I did at (Previous company), where I (result). Glad to share more if helpful.

4. FOLLOW UP DUPA 5 ZILE FARA RASPUNS
Hi (Name), following up on my application for (Job title). I am still very interested. Is there anything else I can send to help your review?

5. CATRE UN VIITOR COLEG (REFERRAL)
Hi (Name), I am applying for (Job title) at (Company) and saw you work in (team). Could I ask you 2 quick questions about the team? 10 minutes, at a time that suits you.

6. CERERE DE REFERRAL DUPA DISCUTIE
Thank you for the chat, (Name). If you think I would be a good fit, would you be open to referring me for (Job title)? Here is my CV and the job link: (link).

7. CATRE HIRING MANAGER
Hi (Name), I follow your team's work on (product or project). I am a (role) who (result). If you plan to grow the team this year, I would love to be on your radar.

8. DUPA INTERVIU (IN 24 DE ORE)
Thank you for your time today, (Name). I enjoyed hearing about (topic from interview). It made me even more excited about (Job title). Looking forward to the next steps.

9. DUPA UN REFUZ
Thank you for letting me know, (Name). I appreciate the process. If a similar role opens at (Company), I would love to be considered. Wishing the team a great quarter.

10. CATRE O COMPANIE FARA JOB DESCHIS
Hi (Name), (Company) is on my list of top remote-first companies. I am a (role) with (result). If you open a role in (team), I would be glad to apply. Here is my profile: (link).

5

Planul de 7 zile

  1. Ziua 1: alege 10 companii din registru care angajeaza rolul tau.
  2. Ziua 2: rescrie CV-ul dupa template.
  3. Ziua 3: fa pasii 1 la 9 din checklist-ul LinkedIn.
  4. Ziua 4: fa pasii 10 la 15 si trimite 10 cereri de conexiune (mesajul 1).
  5. Ziua 5: aplica la 3 joburi si trimite mesajul 3 dupa fiecare aplicatie.
  6. Ziua 6: aplica la inca 3 joburi si cere o discutie unui viitor coleg (mesajul 5).
  7. Ziua 7: trimite follow up (mesajul 4) si noteaza ce raspunsuri ai primit.
