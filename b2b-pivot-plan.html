From Job Seekers to Businesses

5-phase plan to build a B2B audience and sell Yago AI automations through a webinar

The core shift: Stop selling €47–147 digital products to job seekers. Start selling €1,500–5,000 AI automation services to Romanian businesses. Your Instagram becomes a Yago demo channel. Your ads target business owners and decision-makers. Your next webinar speaks to companies drowning in manual processes, not people looking for remote jobs.

Week 1

Clean House

Remove the clutter that's confusing your audience. You don't need to delete your old products — just hide them. The goal is that anyone who finds you sees ONE clear message: AI automation for businesses.

  • Hide old offers from Beacons: Remove the €24 kit, €39 package, €59 English AI, €50 AI Operator page. Don't delete — just unpublish or hide.
  • Update Instagram bio: Change to something like "AI Automation for Romanian Businesses | Yago.ai" with a link to the new B2B landing page.
  • Fix the Flavia Popescu ad account: Appeal the "unusual activity" flag. You need both ad accounts available.
  • Clean up Kit tags: Create new tags for B2B leads (e.g., "b2b-lead", "webinar-business-sept", "yago-interest"). Keep old tags but don't use them for new campaigns.
  • Create a Kit registration form for the B2B webinar (not Zoom-only). You need to track registrations → attendance → sales.
  • Set up Stripe properly: Create clean checkout links for the B2B offer tiers. Test the full flow: checkout → confirmation email → WhatsApp → onboarding.
  • Complete yAGO Terms & Privacy (still has template placeholders per the Growth Reset P0 list).

Weeks 2–5

Build the B2B Audience with Ads

This is the biggest change from August. You need a new audience of business decision-makers, not your existing job-seeker followers. Run two campaign types in parallel: one to build awareness, one to drive webinar registrations.

Campaign 1: Awareness / Lead Magnet (Weeks 2–3)

Targeting: Romanian Business Owners & Managers

Layer these interests and behaviors to reach decision-makers, not employees:

Job Title: CEO, Director, Manager, Fondator Interest: Business automation Interest: Artificial intelligence Interest: n8n / Zapier / Make Interest: CRM software Interest: Recruitment agency Interest: Small business owners Behavior: Business page admins Behavior: Small business owners

Geo: Romania (primary), then expand to RO diaspora in EU if budget allows.
Age: 28–55
Exclusion: Exclude your existing "job seeker" custom audience and current Instagram followers (they're the wrong segment).
Budget: RON 100–150/day for 14 days = RON 1,400–2,100 (~€300–450)
Objective: Lead generation or traffic to a lead magnet landing page

Lead Magnet Offer

Don't send cold traffic straight to a webinar registration. First, offer a free resource that qualifies them as a business:

  • Option A: "Ghidul AI pentru Agentii de Recrutare — 5 Procese pe Care le Poti Automatiza Azi" (PDF guide)
  • Option B: "Audit Gratuit: Cat Timp Pierde Afacerea Ta pe Procese Manuale?" (free quiz/calculator)
  • Option C: "Video Demo: Cum am Automatizat Facturarea pentru o Agentie in 30 de Minute" (gated video)

Collect email + company name + role. Tag them in Kit as "b2b-lead". This builds your B2B email list before the webinar.

Campaign 2: Webinar Registration (Weeks 3–5)

Targeting: Warm B2B Audience + Lookalikes

Now retarget people who engaged with Campaign 1, plus build a Lookalike:

Custom: Lead magnet downloaders Custom: B2B landing page visitors (30d) Lookalike: 1% of lead magnet downloaders Lookalike: 1% of Challenge purchasers

Budget: RON 150–200/day for 14 days = RON 2,100–2,800 (~€450–600)
Objective: Conversions (registration)
Creative: Video ad showing a real automation in action. "In 60 de minute, iti arat cum sa automatizezi 3 procese din afacerea ta. Gratis." Direct to Kit registration form.
Critical: Keep ads running until webinar day. Set end date to the day after. Do NOT let them die early like August.

Week 5–6

The B2B Webinar

Completely different from August 27. This webinar speaks to business owners about their problems, shows live automation demos relevant to their operations, and offers a clear service engagement — not a course.

Webinar Structure (60 min)

0–5'

The Hook

"Cat timp pierde echipa ta pe procese manuale?" Open with a bold stat: "The average Romanian SME wastes 15+ hours/week on tasks AI can handle in minutes." Establish credibility — who you are, what Yago does, clients you've helped.

5–20'

The Problem (3 Business Pain Points)

1. Manual invoicing / document processing eating hours. 2. Lead follow-up falling through the cracks. 3. Content/reporting taking entire days. For each: show the manual way, show the cost (hours x hourly rate = money lost), then tease the automated way.

20–40'

Live Demos (3 Automations)

Demo 1: Invoice Extractor — PDF in, structured data out, synced to accounting. Demo 2: Lead Qualifier — form submission triggers AI scoring + personalized follow-up email. Demo 3: Content Repurposer — one blog post becomes 5 social posts + newsletter. Each demo: 5 min setup, 2 min run, wow moment.

40–50'

The Offer

NOT a course. A done-with-you service engagement:

  • AI Automation Audit (€500): 90-min deep dive into your business processes. Identify top 3 automatable workflows. Deliver a written automation roadmap. This is the entry point — low risk, high value.
  • Implementation Sprint (€2,500): 2-week sprint. We build and deploy 3 custom automations for your business. Includes training for your team. 30 days of support.
  • Monthly Retainer (€500/mo): Ongoing optimization, new automations as needed, priority support, Yago Pro access.

Founding price for webinar attendees only: Audit €500 (normally €1,000). Sprint €2,500 (normally €4,500). Retainer €500/mo (normally €1,000/mo). Valid for 48 hours.

50–60'

Q&A + Close

Answer questions live. Reinforce the 48-hour deadline. CTA: "Book your Audit call" — link to a Calendly or scheduling page, not a Stripe checkout. B2B buyers want a conversation before paying €500+. The call is where you close.
